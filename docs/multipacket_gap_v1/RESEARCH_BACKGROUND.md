## Evidence sufficiency vs missed obligations
Sufficiency checkers (S2G, CRAG, Sufficient Context) judge whether the evidence suffices **for the current hypothesis**. If the first reviewer never considered a norm, a sufficiency check of its reason cannot recover that norm. This study therefore runs a separate channel. **G3B** is a blinded scan over a policy-heavy packet; it sees no verdict and returns verdict-free applicability/condition/exception/violated assessments. **G3V** is a control: the same scan, shown the first verdict. Only scan findings with applicability YES and violated TRUE trigger a recheck, and the recheck must verify conditions and exceptions against the original sources. "Tool available" is never treated as "tool required" (stated in the addendum).
