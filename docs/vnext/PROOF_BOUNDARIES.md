# Proof and certificate boundaries v1

The primitive language has six kinds: observed state, literal result field,
attempted call, completed action, historical action and causal attribution.
It never interprets natural language. Preconditions and implication use explicit
four-valued evidence; FALSE, BOTH and UNKNOWN are not conflated.

Completed actions require a matching unique call/result identity and a T1 effect
regenerated against the exact trusted provider/version/schema contract. T2 proposals
and forged TRUSTED_EFFECT tags do not pass. Causality additionally needs a unique
call reference and the actual claimed state/effect/value, not just a later GET.
Historical action witnesses remain valid after undo/restore. An absence premise
needs explicit closed action-universe and complete-history assumptions, no unknown
or malformed calls/results, no uncaptured user actions and no unknown effects.
Empty retrieval or failed calls alone cannot establish absence/no-effect.

Worlds cover the exact Cartesian product of policy, Goal/Plan, bindings and effect
alternatives. Missing worlds or known material semantic gaps are UNRESOLVED.
There is no voting, confidence threshold or any-error adapter inside the solver.

Certificates hash source plus semantic context, ledger, trusted registry and
problem. The checker does not import/call solver: it reconstructs normalized source
events, regenerates primitive premises, verifies applicable hypothesis/obligation,
checks all worlds and rejects dropped factual/policy safety obligations.

## Empirical semantics are not authoritative facts

An ERROR proof can hold for every supplied admissible interpretation while the
interpretation coverage remains EMPIRICALLY_COVERED. Its certificate explicitly
states that enumeration covers that bounded Phi, not unrestricted NL. Candidate
policy meaning is a proof premise, never a newly observed fact or trusted ontology.
A wrong/missing semantic reading may still invalidate real-world correctness;
stage semantic accuracy and blind confident-wrong audit must measure that risk.
Known uncovered material readings/terms force UNRESOLVED.

NO_ERROR additionally requires provably closed applicable semantic space, complete
source history, complete relevant bindings, material claim coverage and individually
validated absence premises. Empirical challenger silence is insufficient for safety.
An invalid certificate must downgrade the core verdict rather than reaching adapters.

Current deliberate unknown: a T1 guarantee requiring prior state is not proof-checked
using an invented prior snapshot. Without separately grounded prior-state evidence
it remains unproved. Offline executable fixture prior-state metadata is not evidence
about an arbitrary real-world trajectory. This boundary must be included in audits.

Unit checks are not model stage metrics, provider admission or blind improvement.
No result/prompt/configuration in frozen T1 stage v1 was changed by these new modules.
